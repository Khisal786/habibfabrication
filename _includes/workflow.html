How Our CAD-to-Shop Floor Workflow Operates

Three streamlined steps from digital file upload to finished metal part delivery.

Step 01

Upload CAD & Specs

Submit your 2D DXF or 3D STEP files along with material grade, thickness, and quantity requirements through our instant RFQ tool.

Step 02

Precision Fiber Laser Cut

Our 12kW fiber laser systems execute high-speed nesting and clean nitrogen cutting with micron-level positional accuracy.

Step 03

Post-Process & Dispatch

Parts undergo automated deburring, optional press brake bending, and secure industrial packaging for express delivery.
