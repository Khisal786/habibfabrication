AEO & GEO Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

What CAD file formats do you accept for fiber laser cutting?

We accept 2D vector files such as .DXF and .DWG scaled to 1:1, as well as 3D CAD formats including .STEP, .STP, and .SLDPRT for press brake bending and complex assemblies.

What is your standard turnaround time for laser cut parts?

Standard turnaround is 3 to 5 business days. We also offer rapid 24–48 hour express dispatch for urgent prototyping and production runs.

Is there a Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

No. Apex Precision operates with a 0 MOQ policy, supporting single prototype parts up to high-volume production runs of 10,000+ components.

What assist gases do you use for cutting stainless steel and aluminum?

We utilize high-purity, high-pressure nitrogen assist gas for stainless steel and aluminum cutting, ensuring a 100% dross-free, oxide-free, and ready-to-weld edge.
